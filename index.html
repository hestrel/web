Your models. Your workspace.

Hestrel is an iOS app for conversations with AI model providers you configure. Keep your chats together, connect optional tools, and sync conversations with a Hestrel account.

Connect with Google

Choose which services to connect in the app. Hestrel requests read-only access to help answer your questions using your own information.

Gmail
Search and read messages to find information and summarize email.
Google Calendar
Find calendars and events to understand your schedule.
Google Drive
Find files and read Google documents for context in your chats.

These connections cannot send email or change your Google events or files. When a connected service is enabled in a chat, retrieved content is sent to the AI provider selected for that conversation so it can respond.

You can disable services in Hestrel or revoke access in your Google Account. Read our privacy policy for details about data use, storage, and deletion.
